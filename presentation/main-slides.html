Why we added synthetic practice examples

Each training run had only 13 or 14 real students with High burnout. We made synthetic examples by combining answers from similar students so the model could get more practice spotting High burnout.

Amount we triedTraining runsSynthetic examples added per run

Across the 25 runs, we added 132 synthetic examples in total.

What does MAE mean?

Check how many points each guess was off by, whether it was too high or too low. Average those misses across the students we tested. That average is the mean absolute error (MAE).

An MAE of 0.59 means the guesses were off by 0.59 points on average. A smaller MAE means closer guesses.

Where our forest scored higher
Across warning cutoffs, the forest had higher average precision (0.527 vs. 0.423). This measure combines how many High-burnout students a model finds with how often its warnings are correct.

Both models averaged 28 false warnings at their chosen cutoffs. Counts are averages across five repeats. The data leave room for either model to perform better.

Limits / Research presentation

What limits these results

01 / Sample

Convenience sample

The convenience sample included 97 students, 17 of whom met the High cutoff.

02 / Timing

Current symptoms

We measured current symptoms once, so future burnout and causal direction remain unknown.

03 / Selection

Repeated use of the same sample

Earlier model choices used this same sample, which can make performance estimates optimistic.

04 / Generalization

No independent cohort

We still need measured burnout scores from an independent group.

For a future test, we would set the procedure before testing it on new students. This phase has no further recruitment.

Conclusion / Research presentation

What we found

01

GenAI answers lowered ridge error

MAE fell by 0.0300 points in the compact comparison and 0.0211 in the broader one.

02

The forest issued 28 false warnings on average

It also detected 14.8 of the 17 High students on average.

03

Workload alone matched the false-warning count

It detected more High students on average, with paired intervals that include zero.

The next test needs an independent group of students.
